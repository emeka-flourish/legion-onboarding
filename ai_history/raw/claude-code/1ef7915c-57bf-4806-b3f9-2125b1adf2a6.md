- **Booked appointments.** Closest to today's data, but it stops before the goal.

Under the main metric are six stage metrics, from choosing a provider to the step after the visit, with today's numbers where they exist. There are also four guardrails we must not make worse:
- how much the patient types
- time to booking
- whether screened-out people still see helpful resources
- whether a person reviews every safety flag

The 14-day window is an assumption to check against Legion's real availability.
